Frequently Asked Questions

How do I record a game?

Create a new match, add the players or teams, and enter the points for each round. The Application adds up the score automatically and shows the winner when the target score is reached.

Where is my data stored?

Player names, images, scores, settings, and language preferences are stored locally on your device. They are not uploaded to any server. See our Privacy Policy for details.

I lost my games after reinstalling the app. Can they be recovered?

No. Because all data is stored only on your device, uninstalling the Application or clearing its data permanently deletes your match history.

What does the Premium subscription include?

Premium removes all advertisements from the Application. It is an auto-renewable subscription billed through the App Store or Google Play.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple Account or Google Play Account used for the purchase, open the Premium screen in the Application, and tap Restore purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription?

  • iPhone / iPad: Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Domino Apuntes Score > Cancel Subscription.
  • Android: Google Play > profile icon > Payments and subscriptions > Subscriptions > Domino Apuntes Score > Cancel subscription.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period. Premium stays active until the end of the current period.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store that processed your payment, not by us. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com for the App Store, or at Google Play for Android.

I am still seeing ads after subscribing.

Make sure you are signed in with the account used for the purchase, tap Restore purchases on the Premium screen, and restart the Application. If ads continue, contact us with your purchase receipt.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings inside the Application and select your preferred language.

How do I delete all my data?

Clear the Application's data from your device settings, or uninstall the Application. This removes all locally stored information permanently.

How do I report a bug?

Email us at envymarketingsrl@gmail.com with the details listed above. Please report defects within 30 days of discovery, as described in our Terms and Conditions.
